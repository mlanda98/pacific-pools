Where We Work

Serving the Central Valley and Northern California

Pacific Edge Pools is positioned at the center of our service area to efficiently serve both the Central Valley and Northern California. We've built a reputation for quality pool remodeling across a diverse range of markets, from Modesto and Stockton to Danville, Roseville, San Jose, and Napa.

Every market we serve has different water chemistry and climate conditions — from Silicon Valley's very hard 13 GPG water to Folsom Lake's near-pure 1.6 GPG supply, from 110°F desert heat in Ridgecrest to mild coastal fog in Foster City. We know the local conditions in every city we serve and use that knowledge in every finish recommendation we make.
