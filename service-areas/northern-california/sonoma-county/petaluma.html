Local Water Quality

Pool Water Conditions in Petaluma, CA

Water Hardness: ~80 ppm (4.7 GPG) — Moderately Hard Source: Sonoma Water Agency and City of Petaluma, primarily Russian River surface water

Petaluma is served by Sonoma Water's Russian River supply — moderately soft water around 4-5 GPG. Calcium deposits accumulate slowly, and plaster surfaces last longer than in harder-water regions. The mild, coastal-influenced climate adds to Petaluma's favorable pool environment.

Local Climate

Petaluma Climate & Your Pool

Avg. July High: 76°F | Avg. Winter Low: 42°F

Petaluma has a mild coastal climate — summer highs average around 76°F with morning fog common in June and July. The Petaluma Gap channels afternoon winds that moderate summer temperatures. Winters are mild with lows around 42°F and significant winter rainfall.

Petaluma's mild climate and soft water make it one of the most pool-friendly environments in our service area. Pool surface wear is almost entirely driven by age rather than aggressive chemistry or climate. Petaluma pools that are 15–20+ years old typically show the normal wear of aged plaster — ready for professional replastering and a modern finish upgrade rather than emergency repairs.

How hard is the water in Petaluma?

Petaluma's water averages approximately 80 ppm (4.7 GPG) — classified as moderately hard. This affects how quickly calcium scaling develops on tile and how long pool plaster surfaces last.
