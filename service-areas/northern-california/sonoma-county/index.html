Sonoma County Pool Experts

Trusted Pool Remodeling Across Sonoma County

Sonoma County is one of the most desirable regions in California — wine country character, beautiful landscapes, and homeowners who care about the quality of their properties. Pacific Edge Pools serves Petaluma, Santa Rosa, and Windsor with professional pool remodeling, replastering, resurfacing, tile & coping, skimmer installs, and mastic work.

Sonoma County cities are served by Sonoma Water Agency's Russian River supply — moderately soft water around 4–7 GPG. Santa Rosa runs slightly harder (6–7 GPG) due to groundwater supplementation. Pool surface wear in Sonoma County is driven more by the age of the pool stock and moderate summer heat than by aggressive hard water scaling. The mild, pool-friendly climate means surfaces last longer here, but 15–20+ year old pools throughout the county are ready for proper renovation.
