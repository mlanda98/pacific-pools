Alameda County Pool Experts

Trusted Pool Remodeling Across Alameda County

Alameda County spans the full range of Bay Area climate and water conditions — from the mild, foggy shoreline cities to the warm inland Tri-Valley corridor. Pacific Edge Pools serves all of Alameda County with professional pool remodeling, replastering, resurfacing, tile & coping, skimmer installs, and mastic work.

Water hardness varies significantly across Alameda County — EBMUD-served cities like Hayward and Fremont have moderately hard water around 2–9 GPG, while Zone 7-served Tri-Valley cities like Livermore and Pleasanton range from 10–19 GPG depending on the season and groundwater usage. Hard water is the primary driver of pool surface wear in most of the county.
