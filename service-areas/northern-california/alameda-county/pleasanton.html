Local Water Quality

Pool Water Conditions in Pleasanton, CA

Water Hardness: ~200 ppm (11.7 GPG) — Very Hard Source: Zone 7 Water Agency blend of surface water and Livermore-Amador Valley groundwater, with demineralization at Mocho Wellfield

Pleasanton is served by Zone 7 with water ranging from 7 to 19 GPG depending on season — averaging around 11-12 GPG. Zone 7 operates a demineralization plant but hardness climbs significantly in dry years. Pool owners in Pleasanton regularly deal with calcium deposits on tile and the gradual surface roughness that hard water causes on standard plaster finishes.

Local Climate

Pleasanton Climate & Your Pool

Avg. July High: 88°F | Avg. Winter Low: 42°F

Pleasanton has a warm inland East Bay climate — summers are noticeably warmer than coastal cities with July highs around 88°F and inland heat events regularly pushing 98–105°F. Winters are mild with lows around 42°F.

Pleasanton's combination of hard water and warm summers creates pool surface conditions more like the Central Valley than the coast. High temperatures drive evaporation, concentrating the mineral content, and the thermal cycling puts stress on plaster and expansion joints. Pleasanton is among the most actively remodeled pool markets in the East Bay.
