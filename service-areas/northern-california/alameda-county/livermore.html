Local Water Quality

Pool Water Conditions in Livermore, CA

Water Hardness: ~180 ppm (10.5 GPG) — Very Hard Source: Zone 7 Water Agency blend of State Water Project surface water and Livermore-Amador Valley groundwater

Livermore is served by Zone 7 Water Agency, blending State Water Project surface water with local groundwater. Hardness varies by season — averaging around 10-11 GPG but spiking higher in dry years when more groundwater is used. This makes Livermore one of the harder-water East Bay cities. Pool owners see calcium scaling on tile and accelerated plaster wear that closely resembles what we see in the Central Valley.

Local Climate

Livermore Climate & Your Pool

Avg. July High: 88°F | Avg. Winter Low: 42°F

Livermore sits in the Livermore Valley, inland from the Bay fog belt — summers are significantly warmer than coastal East Bay cities. July highs average around 88°F with heat spikes to 100–105°F during inland heat events. Winters are mild with lows around 42°F.

Livermore's inland location makes it the warmest of the East Bay cities, combining hard water with Valley-like heat. High summer temperatures drive evaporation and mineral concentration, and the thermal stress from hot summers to mild winters puts gradual pressure on plaster and expansion joints. Livermore pool conditions are closer to the Central Valley than to the coast.
