Local Water Quality

Pool Water Conditions in El Dorado Hills, CA

Water Hardness: ~95 ppm (5.6 GPG) — Moderately Hard Source: El Dorado Irrigation District (EID), surface water from Sly Park Reservoir and American River

El Dorado Hills shares Cameron Park's water from EID — moderately hard around 5-6 GPG. Calcium scaling is less aggressive than the Central Valley, but El Dorado Hills pools still develop tile deposits and plaster wear over time, especially in areas with local groundwater supplements.

Local Climate

El Dorado Hills Climate & Your Pool

Avg. July High: 91°F | Avg. Winter Low: 38°F

El Dorado Hills has a warm foothill climate with more seasonal variation than the Valley floor — July highs around 91°F with peaks to 98–103°F, and winters with genuine lows around 38°F with occasional frost and more precipitation.

El Dorado Hills is one of the fastest-growing affluent communities in the Sacramento region, with newer pool stock that still benefits from replastering at the 7–10 year mark. The foothill climate means expansion joints and coping take more stress than in flat-valley locations. A thorough mastic install is essential for El Dorado Hills remodels that need to hold up through multiple winter seasons.
