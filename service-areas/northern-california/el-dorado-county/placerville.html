Local Water Quality

Pool Water Conditions in Placerville, CA

Water Hardness: ~105 ppm (6.1 GPG) — Moderately Hard Source: El Dorado Irrigation District and local well water from the El Dorado County foothill aquifer

Placerville draws from EID surface water and local groundwater, with hardness around 6 GPG. Pool owners see gradual calcium tile deposits and plaster wear consistent with moderately hard water, combined with foothill thermal cycling.

Local Climate

Placerville Climate & Your Pool

Avg. July High: 90°F | Avg. Winter Low: 37°F

Placerville has a true foothill climate — warm summers with July highs around 90°F and occasional peaks to 98°F, and genuine cool winters with lows around 37°F and frequent frost. The area receives significant winter rainfall and occasional snow.

Placerville's genuine cool winters and warm summers create meaningful freeze-thaw cycling that pool contractors from flat valley areas often underestimate. Pool plaster, coping, and mastic joints are vulnerable to freeze expansion damage — a leading cause of coping separation and surface cracking in older Placerville pools.
