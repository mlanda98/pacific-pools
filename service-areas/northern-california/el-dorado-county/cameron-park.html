Local Water Quality

Pool Water Conditions in Cameron Park, CA

Water Hardness: ~95 ppm (5.6 GPG) — Moderately Hard Source: El Dorado Irrigation District (EID), primarily Sly Park Reservoir and American River surface water

Cameron Park is served by El Dorado Irrigation District with moderately hard water around 5-6 GPG from surface water. This is softer than the Central Valley but hard enough to cause noticeable calcium deposits on pool tile over time. The foothill geology adds some additional mineral content compared to the flat valley floor.

Local Climate

Cameron Park Climate & Your Pool

Avg. July High: 91°F | Avg. Winter Low: 38°F

Cameron Park has a classic Sierra foothill climate — warm dry summers with July highs around 91°F and peak days reaching 98–102°F. Winters are noticeably cooler than the Valley floor with lows around 38°F and occasional frost.

Cameron Park's foothill location introduces thermal cycling that flat-valley cities don't experience — cooler winters mean more expansion and contraction stress on plaster, coping, and mastic joints. The combination of moderate water hardness and genuine cool-season temperatures is why we see more coping separation and joint stress in Cameron Park pools than in warmer, flatter locations.
