Local Water Quality

Pool Water Conditions in Elk Grove, CA

Water Hardness: ~120 ppm (7.0 GPG) — Hard Source: Sacramento County Water Agency and EGWD, primarily Sacramento River surface water with groundwater supplementation

Elk Grove draws from Sacramento River surface water and local groundwater — hardness in the hard range around 7 GPG. The city has grown dramatically and has a large inventory of 10–20 year old pools now entering the prime remodeling window. Calcium scaling on tile and gradual plaster wear are common findings throughout the city.

Local Climate

Elk Grove Climate & Your Pool

Avg. July High: 96°F | Avg. Winter Low: 39°F

Elk Grove has a hot Sacramento Valley climate — July highs average around 96°F with peak days regularly reaching 103–108°F. The flat valley floor location provides no topographic relief from the summer heat. Winters are mild with lows around 39°F and occasional dense tule fog.

Elk Grove's flat valley location and hot summers drive significant evaporation and thermal stress on pool surfaces — comparable to the Central Valley. Hard water combined with Valley heat produces the same degradation patterns we see throughout the Central Valley. Elk Grove is an active and growing pool remodeling market.
