Local Water Quality

Pool Water Conditions in Fairfield, CA

Water Hardness: ~145 ppm (8.5 GPG) — Hard Source: Fairfield-Suisun utilities, Sacramento River surface water treated at Barker Slough

Fairfield draws primarily from Sacramento River surface water — hard water around 8-9 GPG. Pool owners see calcium tile scaling, gradual plaster roughness, and waterline staining consistent with the greater Sacramento Valley supply region. Travis Air Force Base brings a rotating homeowner population who may not be familiar with Northern California hard water conditions.

Local Climate

Fairfield Climate & Your Pool

Avg. July High: 91°F | Avg. Winter Low: 40°F

Fairfield has a distinctive climate influenced by the Carquinez Strait — summer highs average around 91°F with peak days reaching 100–106°F, but strong afternoon winds can provide some relief. Winters are mild with lows around 40°F.

Fairfield's hot, windy summers drive significant pool evaporation — the afternoon winds actually accelerate water loss and mineral concentration beyond what temperature alone would cause. This compounds the hard water scaling issue and can degrade pool chemistry faster than homeowners expect. The heat-wind combination is why we see faster tile scaling and plaster wear in Fairfield than the 8-9 GPG hardness number alone would suggest.
