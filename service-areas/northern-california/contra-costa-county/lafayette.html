Local Water Quality

Pool Water Conditions in Lafayette, CA

Water Hardness: ~63 ppm (3.7 GPG) — Soft Source: East Bay Municipal Utility District (EBMUD), Mokelumne River watershed Sierra snowmelt

Lafayette is served by EBMUD with exceptionally soft water — around 3-4 GPG from the Mokelumne River. Calcium scaling is minimal, and plaster surfaces last longer here than in harder-water cities. However, very soft water is slightly aggressive toward plaster chemistry — without careful pH and alkalinity management, it can slowly etch and dissolve plaster rather than depositing scale. Water chemistry maintenance matters more in Lafayette than in hard-water cities.

Local Climate

Lafayette Climate & Your Pool

Avg. July High: 84°F | Avg. Winter Low: 43°F

Lafayette has a classic inland East Bay hills climate — warmer than bayshore cities due to its hillside position, with July highs averaging around 84°F and heat events pushing 95–100°F. Winters are mild with lows around 43°F and moderate rainfall.

Lafayette's mild climate and soft water create pool conditions where chemistry management matters more than finish choice. Properly balanced soft water is pool-friendly; improperly balanced soft water is aggressive toward plaster. When we replaster a Lafayette pool, we discuss water chemistry maintenance with the homeowner — it directly affects how long the new surface lasts.
