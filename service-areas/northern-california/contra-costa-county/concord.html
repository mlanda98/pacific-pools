Local Water Quality

Pool Water Conditions in Concord, CA

Water Hardness: ~155 ppm (9.1 GPG) — Hard Source: Contra Costa Water District, primarily Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta surface water via Los Vaqueros Reservoir

Concord is served by Contra Costa Water District, drawing from the Sacramento Delta via Los Vaqueros Reservoir. This surface water supply gives Concord moderately hard water — around 9 GPG — softer than Zone 7 Tri-Valley cities but still hard enough to cause calcium scaling on pool tile and gradual plaster wear over time.

Local Climate

Concord Climate & Your Pool

Avg. July High: 87°F | Avg. Winter Low: 44°F

Concord has a warm inland East Bay climate — protected from coastal fog by the Diablo Range, it gets significantly more summer heat. July highs average around 87°F with peak days reaching 95–102°F. Winters are mild with lows around 44°F.

Concord's inland location means pool surfaces face more heat stress than coastal East Bay cities. Summer temperatures drive evaporation and mineral concentration, and the warm-dry summers combined with wet winters create seasonal soil movement that stresses pool deck expansion joints. Moderately hard water and inland heat make proper replastering and mastic work important for any Concord pool.
