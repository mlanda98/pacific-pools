Local Water Quality

Pool Water Conditions in San Ramon, CA

Water Hardness: ~185 ppm (10.8 GPG) — Very Hard Source: Zone 7 Water Agency blend of State Water Project surface water and Livermore-Amador Valley groundwater

San Ramon is served by Zone 7, sharing the same variable hard water profile as Danville and Pleasanton — averaging around 10-11 GPG with seasonal spikes when Zone 7 relies more heavily on groundwater. San Ramon's rapid growth has produced a large stock of 10–20 year old pools now entering the prime replastering window.

Local Climate

San Ramon Climate & Your Pool

Avg. July High: 87°F | Avg. Winter Low: 42°F

San Ramon has a warm inland Tri-Valley climate — July highs average around 87°F with peak days reaching 100–105°F. The city is largely sheltered from Bay Area fog. Winters are mild with lows around 42°F.

San Ramon's inland warmth combined with hard water creates pool conditions closer to the Central Valley than the coast. Summer heat drives evaporation and mineral concentration, and the Tri-Valley clay soils expand and contract seasonally stressing mastic joints. San Ramon is one of our most active East Bay service areas.

How hard is the water in San Ramon?

San Ramon's water averages approximately 185 ppm (10.8 GPG) — classified as very hard. This affects how quickly calcium scaling develops on tile and how long pool plaster surfaces last.
