Local Water Quality

Pool Water Conditions in Santa Clara, CA

Water Hardness: ~238 ppm (13.9 GPG) — Very Hard Source: Santa Clara city water and San Jose Water Company, Santa Clara Valley groundwater with State Water Project supplements

Santa Clara is served by city utilities and SJWC with the same very hard water profile as the broader Silicon Valley — around 13-14 GPG. The Santa Clara Valley sits on a deep groundwater basin rich in calcium and magnesium. Pool tile scaling, plaster deterioration, and waterline deposits are standard findings in Santa Clara pools.

Local Climate

Santa Clara Climate & Your Pool

Avg. July High: 82°F | Avg. Winter Low: 46°F

Santa Clara has a mild South Bay climate — July highs average around 82°F with occasional inland heat events pushing 95–100°F. The coastal influence from the Santa Cruz Mountains moderates temperatures more than inland Valley cities. Winters are mild with lows around 46°F.

Santa Clara's very hard water is the dominant driver of pool surface wear. The moderate climate means heat stress is secondary, but 13-14 GPG hard water is aggressive enough on its own to significantly shorten the life of standard white plaster and cause heavy tile scaling. Quartz and pebble finishes are strongly recommended.
