Local Water Quality

Pool Water Conditions in Rocklin, CA

Water Hardness: ~78 ppm (4.6 GPG) — Moderately Hard Source: Rocklin municipal water, sourcing from the American River through Placer County Water Agency

Rocklin shares the moderately hard water profile of neighboring Roseville and Lincoln — around 4-5 GPG from American River surface water. Calcium scaling accumulates gradually, and plaster wear is driven more by hot Sacramento summers than by water chemistry. Rocklin's rapid growth has produced a large inventory of 10–20 year old pools in the remodeling window.

Local Climate

Rocklin Climate & Your Pool

Avg. July High: 96°F | Avg. Winter Low: 39°F

Rocklin has a warm Sacramento foothill climate — July highs average around 96°F with peak days regularly reaching 103–107°F. Winters are mild with lows around 39°F.

Rocklin's hot summers are the dominant factor in pool surface wear. At 96°F average July highs and regular 103°F+ peaks, the thermal stress and evaporation are significant. Even 8–10 year old pools in newer Rocklin communities show surface wear from the combination of heat and moderate mineral content.
