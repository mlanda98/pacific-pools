Local Water Quality

Pool Water Conditions in Folsom, CA

Water Hardness: ~28 ppm (1.6 GPG) — Soft Source: City of Folsom Water Treatment Plant, 100% surface water from Folsom Lake (American River Sierra snowmelt)

Folsom has some of the softest water in Northern California — around 1.6 GPG — because 100% comes from Folsom Lake fed by Sierra Nevada snowmelt. Calcium scaling is minimal. However, very soft water can be slightly aggressive toward pool plaster if pH and alkalinity aren't carefully maintained.

Local Climate

Folsom Climate & Your Pool

Avg. July High: 95°F | Avg. Winter Low: 39°F

Folsom has a warm Sacramento foothill climate — summer highs average around 95°F with peak days reaching 102–107°F during heat events. Winters are mild with lows around 39°F.

Folsom's combination of very soft water and hot summers creates a specific pool wear profile: minimal calcium scaling but significant heat stress on plaster surfaces. At 95°F+ average July highs, Folsom pools face the same thermal stress as Central Valley pools. The soft water means the chemistry challenge is keeping the pool from being aggressive toward the plaster, not managing scale.
