Local Water Quality

Pool Water Conditions in Ridgecrest, CA

Water Hardness: ~250 ppm (14.6 GPG) — Very Hard Source: primarily groundwater from the Indian Wells Valley desert aquifer

Ridgecrest sits in the Mojave Desert's Indian Wells Valley, and its groundwater is among the hardest in California — approximately 14-15 GPG. The desert geology produces extremely mineral-rich groundwater with high calcium and magnesium content, causing aggressive calcium scaling on pool tile, rapid deterioration of standard plaster, and intense waterline staining. Pools in Ridgecrest need more frequent tile cleaning and surface maintenance than Valley pools, and standard white plaster is a poor choice here. Quartz and pebble finishes are strongly recommended. The extreme desert heat — summer temperatures regularly exceeding 110°F — compounds the problem significantly.

Local Climate

Ridgecrest Climate & Your Pool

Avg. July High: 104°F | Avg. Winter Low: 38°F

Ridgecrest is in the Mojave Desert, and its climate is extreme — July highs average 104°F and peak days regularly exceed 110–115°F. The city receives only about 5 inches of rain per year, almost entirely in winter. Winters are cool with lows around 38°F but rarely freezing. The low humidity and intense sun are defining characteristics of the desert climate.

Ridgecrest has the most demanding climate for pool surfaces in our entire service area. The combination of extreme desert heat (104°F average July highs, peaks above 110°F), very low humidity (20-30% in summer), intense UV exposure, and some of the hardest water in California creates conditions that degrade pool surfaces faster than almost anywhere else. Evaporation is aggressive — a Ridgecrest pool can lose several inches of water per week in peak summer — concentrating the already very-hard water rapidly. Standard white plaster typically lasts only 5–7 years in Ridgecrest conditions compared to 10+ years in moderate-climate cities. Quartz or pebble finishes are not just recommended here — they're essentially mandatory for any remodel expected to last.
