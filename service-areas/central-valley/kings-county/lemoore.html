Local Water Quality

Pool Water Conditions in Lemoore, CA

Water Hardness: ~155 ppm (9.1 GPG) — Hard Source: groundwater from Kings County aquifers near the former Tulare Lake bed

Lemoore sits near the former Tulare Lake bed, an area with some of the deepest and most mineral-rich agricultural soils in California. Groundwater here tests in the hard range — approximately 9 GPG — and pool owners see consistent calcium scaling on tile and waterline surfaces, as well as the gradual roughening of plaster finishes that comes with hard water over time. We find that explaining the local water chemistry to homeowners helps set expectations for how often a Lemoore pool will need tile cleaning and surface maintenance compared to softer-water regions.

Local Climate

Lemoore Climate & Your Pool

Avg. July High: 99°F | Avg. Winter Low: 39°F

Lemoore has the same intense valley climate as neighboring Hanford — July highs average 99°F with peak days frequently reaching 107–110°F. The flat Kings County location offers no topographical cooling relief. Winters bring lows around 39°F and the tule fog characteristic of the Central Valley.

Lemoore pools face the same double challenge as most southern San Joaquin Valley cities: hard water and extreme heat. The 99°F average summer highs drive significant evaporation, concentrating minerals rapidly and accelerating the scaling process. The thermal cycling between hot summers and cool, foggy winters puts expansion and contraction stress on plaster finishes and coping. For the Naval Air Station community in Lemoore, many homeowners come from other parts of the country and aren't aware of how quickly the local climate and water chemistry can degrade a pool surface — setting proper expectations is something we make a point of doing on every Lemoore estimate.
