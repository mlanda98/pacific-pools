Local Water Quality

Pool Water Conditions in Merced, CA

Water Hardness: ~157 ppm (9.2 GPG) — Hard Source: groundwater from the Merced subbasin of the San Joaquin Valley

Merced's water tests at approximately 9.2 GPG — classified as hard — with groundwater drawn from the Merced subbasin. This level of hardness causes visible calcium scaling on pool tile and waterline surfaces, and contributes to the rough texture and chalky discoloration that develops on older plaster finishes over time. While not as extreme as Modesto or Manteca, Merced's hard water still accelerates plaster wear and tile staining compared to softer-water regions. We recommend quartz or pebble surfaces for Merced pools — they're more resistant to mineral etching and will look better for longer than standard white plaster in these conditions.

Local Climate

Merced Climate & Your Pool

Avg. July High: 96°F | Avg. Winter Low: 39°F

Merced has a classic hot valley climate — July highs average around 96°F with peak days frequently reaching 103–107°F. The city gets almost no rainfall from June through September and has a dry, sunny summer season. Winters bring cool temperatures, lows in the upper 30s, and the thick tule fog characteristic of the Central Valley.

Merced's intense summer heat accelerates pool evaporation and concentrates the minerals in the pool water, compounding the hard water scaling that's already happening from the groundwater supply. The fog season also introduces a sustained wet period in winter that contrasts sharply with the dry summer — this seasonal soil moisture cycle causes movement in the clay soils common around Merced that can stress pool decks and coping over time. Properly sealed expansion joints protect against water intrusion beneath the deck during the wet months.
