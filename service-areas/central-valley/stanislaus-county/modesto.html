Local Water Quality

Pool Water Conditions in Modesto, CA

Water Hardness: ~211 ppm (12.3 GPG) — Very Hard Source: primarily groundwater from deep aquifers beneath the Tuolumne River watershed

At 12.3 GPG, Modesto has some of the hardest water in the Central Valley. Calcium scale builds up quickly on pool tile and waterline surfaces, and standard white plaster deteriorates faster here than almost anywhere else in the region. Quartz and pebble finishes are strongly recommended for Modesto pools — they resist the mineral etching that hard water accelerates. When we replaster a Modesto pool, we also acid-wash and scale-strip the existing tile before applying any new finish to ensure proper adhesion over the mineral-coated shell.

Local Climate

Modesto Climate & Your Pool

Avg. July High: 98°F | Avg. Winter Low: 40°F

Modesto summers are long and dry — July averages 98°F highs with temperatures regularly pushing 104–108°F on peak days. The city receives almost no rainfall from June through September. Winters are cool with lows around 40°F and occasional valley fog.

The intense summer heat drives rapid evaporation, which concentrates calcium and other minerals in pool water — accelerating the scaling and surface wear that Modesto's already-hard water causes on its own. Pool surfaces in Modesto are essentially under attack from two directions at once: hard water from below and heat from above. The combination shortens the lifespan of standard white plaster significantly compared to what manufacturers' ratings would suggest. A quartz or pebble finish handles both factors better, and properly sealed mastic expansion joints protect the deck from seasonal ground movement caused by the dry-summer, wet-winter soil cycle.
