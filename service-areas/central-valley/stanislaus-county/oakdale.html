Local Water Quality

Pool Water Conditions in Oakdale, CA

Water Hardness: ~195 ppm (11.4 GPG) — Very Hard Source: groundwater from Stanislaus County aquifers fed by the Stanislaus River watershed

Oakdale's groundwater is drawn from the same mineral-rich aquifer system that gives most of Stanislaus County its hard water character. At roughly 11+ GPG, Oakdale water produces the same calcium scaling and plaster degradation issues we see throughout the county. Oakdale also sits on the eastern edge of the county where the transition from Valley floor to foothill geology means slight variations in mineral content by neighborhood. Either way, quartz and pebble finishes hold up far better here than standard plaster over the long term.

Local Climate

Oakdale Climate & Your Pool

Avg. July High: 97°F | Avg. Winter Low: 40°F

Oakdale's climate is similar to the rest of Stanislaus County — hot, dry summers with July highs averaging 97°F and temperatures regularly topping 103°F. Located slightly further east toward the foothills, Oakdale gets marginally more variation than the valley floor. Winters are cool with lows around 40°F.

The heat in Oakdale drives the same pool surface stress patterns we see throughout the county — rapid evaporation concentrating minerals, hard water scaling on tile and plaster, and mastic joints drying out and cracking in the summer heat. Oakdale pools that haven't been remodeled in 10+ years typically show all of these issues simultaneously. The eastern location also means pools near the foothill transition zone can experience slightly more soil movement than those on the valley floor, making proper coping and mastic work especially important.
