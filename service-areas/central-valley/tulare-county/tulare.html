Local Water Quality

Pool Water Conditions in Visalia, CA

Water Hardness: ~77 ppm (4.5 GPG) — Moderately Hard Source: groundwater from 58 Cal Water wells drawing from the Kaweah River alluvial fan

According to Cal Water's 2023 consumer confidence report, Visalia has an average water hardness of 77 ppm (about 4.5 GPG) — moderately hard, and noticeably softer than most of the Central Valley. This is good news for pools: calcium scaling is less aggressive in Visalia than in harder-water cities, and standard plaster finishes hold up longer before showing mineral wear. That said, Visalia's extreme summer heat — temperatures regularly exceeding 105°F — is still a significant factor in pool surface degradation. The thermal cycling from hot summers and mild winters causes plaster cracking and coping stress over time, even without severe hard water.

Local Climate

Visalia Climate & Your Pool

Avg. July High: 98°F | Avg. Winter Low: 40°F

Visalia has an intensely hot summer climate — July highs average 98°F and peak days frequently reach 106–110°F, making it one of the hottest cities in the San Joaquin Valley. The city gets almost no rain from May through September. Winters bring cool temperatures, lows around 40°F, and persistent valley fog from December through February.

Despite Visalia's relatively soft water, the extreme summer heat is a significant driver of pool surface wear. At 98°F average July highs, pool evaporation is aggressive — even soft water concentrates over a hot summer, and the thermal stress from peak-summer temperatures to cool winter nights puts repeated expansion and contraction stress on plaster surfaces and coping. The fog season also creates prolonged wet conditions for pool decks during winter, which is when improperly sealed mastic joints allow water infiltration beneath the deck. In Visalia, the climate matters as much as the water chemistry in determining how long a pool finish will last.
