Local Knowledge

Pool Conditions in Manteca

Manteca has some of the hardest water in San Joaquin County — averaging around 16 grains per gallon, well into the "very hard" classification. This causes accelerated calcium scaling on pool tile, waterline staining, and premature deterioration of standard white plaster surfaces. If you've noticed a rough, chalky texture on your pool walls, or white crusty buildup along the waterline tile, Manteca's hard water is almost certainly the cause. When we replaster or resurface pools in Manteca, we specifically recommend quartz aggregate or pebble finishes over standard white plaster — these denser surfaces resist the etching and mineral penetration that hard water accelerates. We also remove calcium scale from existing tile as part of our tile & coping work before any new surface is applied.

Why is my Manteca pool plaster rough and stained?

Hard water is the most common cause in Manteca. Local water is high in calcium and magnesium, which causes mineral scaling on tile and surface deterioration on older plaster. Quartz or pebble finishes hold up significantly better in these conditions.
