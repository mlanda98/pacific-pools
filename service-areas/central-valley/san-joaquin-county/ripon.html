Local Knowledge

Pool Conditions in Ripon

Ripon's water supply shares the hard water characteristics common to eastern San Joaquin County — elevated calcium and magnesium levels that cause calcium scaling on tile, surface roughness on older plaster finishes, and waterline staining over time. Pebble Tec, Pebble Sheen, and quartz aggregate finishes are significantly more resistant to hard water than standard plaster — but only when applied correctly over a properly prepared shell. Our team has the experience to do this right. We also pay close attention to coping and mastic work in Ripon, where larger lot sizes and shifting clay soils can cause deck movement that stresses expansion joints over time. A properly done mastic install protects the pool structure and prevents water from getting beneath the deck.

Why is my Ripon pool plaster rough and stained?

Hard water is the most common cause in Ripon. Local water is high in calcium and magnesium, which causes mineral scaling on tile and surface deterioration on older plaster. Quartz or pebble finishes hold up significantly better in these conditions.

Basic replastering starts around $5,000–$10,000. Full remodels with new tile, coping, skimmer installs, and mastic work typically range from $15,000–$40,000+. We offer free estimates with no obligation.
