Local Knowledge

Pool Conditions in Escalon

Escalon's water supply shares the hard water characteristics of much of eastern San Joaquin County — elevated calcium and magnesium levels that cause accelerated mineral scaling on pool surfaces and tile. Escalon also sits on agricultural land where seasonal soil movement is common, which can stress pool decks, expansion joints, and coping over time. We frequently see mastic failure in Escalon pools — where the flexible expansion joint sealant between the pool coping and the deck has dried, cracked, or pulled away — allowing water to penetrate beneath the deck and cause further movement. Proper mastic installation is one of the most overlooked parts of a pool renovation, and it's something we take seriously on every Escalon project. Combined with the right plaster or pebble finish for the local water chemistry, a properly done remodel in Escalon will last 15–20 years with normal maintenance.

Why is my Escalon pool plaster rough and stained?

Hard water is the most common cause in Escalon. Local water is high in calcium and magnesium, which causes mineral scaling on tile and surface deterioration on older plaster. Quartz or pebble finishes hold up significantly better in these conditions.
