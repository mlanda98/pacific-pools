Local Knowledge

Pool Conditions in Stockton

One of the most common issues we see in Stockton pools is calcium scaling and accelerated plaster deterioration. Stockton has notoriously hard water — high in calcium and magnesium minerals — which causes white scale buildup on tile, waterline staining, and premature breakdown of plaster surfaces. If your Stockton pool plaster is rough, discolored, or flaking, hard water is often the culprit. When we replaster or resurface a Stockton pool, we take the local water chemistry into account and recommend finishes that hold up better in high-mineral environments — like quartz aggregate and pebble surfaces, which resist etching and scaling far better than standard white plaster.

Why is my Stockton pool plaster rough and stained?

Hard water is the most common cause in Stockton. Local water is high in calcium and magnesium, which causes mineral scaling on tile and surface deterioration on older plaster. Quartz or pebble finishes hold up significantly better in these conditions.
